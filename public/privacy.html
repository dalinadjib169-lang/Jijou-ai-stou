Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to AI Dali Emploi en CEM. This application is an AI-powered educational assistant designed to help students by answering their academic questions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe educational environment.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

  • No account creation is required to use the app.
  • We do not request access to your device's Location, Contacts, Camera, or Microphone.
  • The application only processes the text questions you submit in order to generate an AI response.

How We Use Information

The app requires internet access strictly for the purpose of sending your text queries to our AI service and returning the educational response to your screen. The questions you ask are processed in real-time and are not linked to your identity or stored for marketing purposes.

Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information or require user accounts, there is no personal profile data at risk of being breached. All communication between the app and the AI generation servers is encrypted using standard internet security protocols (HTTPS) to ensure your queries remain private in transit.

Children's Privacy

As an educational tool, this app is designed to be safe for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such data.
